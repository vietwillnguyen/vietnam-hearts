## Why no address, hours or phone number

The school's address is given out only after someone has signed up through the volunteer form and joined the Zalo group.
A public page that collects a drop-off intention has to answer "where do I bring it" without answering it, or every submitter asks and somebody eventually replies in a hurry.
So three surfaces carry the same line and nothing carries more: this website block, the form's "who delivers" help text, and the acknowledgement email.
The public geography is the district, `Thạnh Mỹ Tây, TP.HCM`, and nothing finer.

The same reasoning covers opening hours, which would imply a place to turn up to, and a phone number, which belongs on a surface a human controls rather than on a page that is scraped.
