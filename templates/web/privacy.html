Privacy Policy: Vietnam Hearts Volunteer Management

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

Vietnam Hearts Volunteer Management (the "Portal") is the administrative tool Vietnam Hearts uses to manage volunteer activities. Its dashboard is restricted to authorised administrators. A few pages are public - this policy, the sign-in page, and the email-preferences page linked from the emails we send - and the Portal also handles messages that members of the public send to our volunteer inbox and to our Facebook Page.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the administrators who sign in to the Portal, the volunteers whose information is stored in it, and anyone who writes to our volunteer inbox or messages our Facebook Page.

Information we collect

  • Administrator account information: the name and email address of the Google account used to sign in. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth and is limited to an allow-list of authorised addresses.
  • Volunteer information: details submitted through our volunteer sign-up form or entered into the Portal - name, email address, phone number, location, availability, start date and intended commitment, teaching experience and certificates, Vietnamese proficiency, the roles and support offered, and a record of the emails we have sent you.
  • Correspondence: the messages you send to our volunteer inbox or to our Facebook Page, including their content.
  • Technical data: server logs generated automatically when the Portal is used - the request method and path, the client IP address, the browser type and other request headers, and timestamps. Authorization headers and cookies are stripped before anything is written to a log.

How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Operate and secure the Portal
  • Coordinate and communicate with volunteers
  • Manage volunteer scheduling and records for Vietnam Hearts
  • Triage, answer and follow up on messages sent to our volunteer inbox

Volunteer inbox assistant

The Portal connects to the Vietnam Hearts volunteer inbox ({{ contact_email }}) using Google's Gmail API with the gmail.modify permission. With that access the Portal:

  • applies labels to incoming mail so each message's status is visible in the inbox;
  • saves a suggested reply as a Gmail draft for a person to review;
  • forwards mail that needs a person to a Vietnam Hearts organiser; and
  • posts a short notice to an internal Vietnam Hearts Discord channel so an organiser sees it quickly. That notice carries the message's category, how urgent it is, and a link to the thread in Gmail. It does not carry the sender's address or the body of the message; where a one-sentence summary is included, it is generated with the explicit instruction to leave out names and contact details.

To categorise a message and to draft a reply, the Portal sends the message's content to the AI services it uses for that purpose: Jev (TypeSafe AI), which classifies the message, and Google Gemini, which drafts replies and summaries. A second classification runs in parallel for comparison through LiteLLM, which routes to Google Gemini by default and can be configured to use Anthropic Claude instead. Message content is sent to these services only to triage messages and draft replies. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except:

  • Service providers that host or support the Portal: Google Cloud, which hosts the Portal on Cloud Run; Google Workspace, where our volunteer sign-up responses and class schedule are held in Google Sheets; Supabase, which hosts the Portal's database and provides sign-in; Gmail, which sends the Portal's email; Jev (TypeSafe AI), Google Gemini and LiteLLM, as described above; Discord, which receives the short internal notices described above; Meta, which delivers messages sent to our Facebook Page; and Sentry, which receives error reports and, when an error occurs, the IP address and headers of the request that caused it.
  • Where required by law

Data storage and security

The Portal runs on Google Cloud Run. Its database is PostgreSQL hosted by Supabase, and volunteer sign-up responses and the class schedule are held in Google Sheets. We use reasonable safeguards, including restricted, authenticated access limited to an allow-list of administrator accounts, to protect it. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

Information is kept only as long as needed for volunteer management. It is deleted on request or when no longer needed.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us below.

Children

The Portal is not intended for use by children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information through it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
